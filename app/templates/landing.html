How it stays right

The AI talks.
The system decides.

A language model is great at conversation and bad at calendars. In Recepta the model never decides whether a time is free. Every request goes through a booking engine with your clinic's rules.

  • Opening hours, lunch and notice periods are enforced on every booking, by code.
  • Chairs and visit lengths: a 60-minute cleaning is refused if it would overlap a full slot.
  • No double booking, ever. A PostgreSQL exclusion constraint rejects two overlapping visits on one chair, even when two callers grab the same time at the same second.
  • Staff blocks in Google Calendar (meetings, days off) disappear from what Ava offers.
  • Identity before changes: callers only see visits tied to their own number, or the code from their text.
  • Medical safety: dangerous symptoms get a 911 instruction, never a booking.
